# Domain Plan

Research date: October 10, 2026

## Recommended Primary Domain

Use **domaindatabases.com** as the main domain, with **domains-database.com** as its strongest redirect alias. It combines clear domain inventory meaning with a clean, easy-to-share address.

Google says keywords in a domain name have very little direct ranking effect. The ranking below therefore weighs relevance to the app, memorability, and attracting the right audience. Actual SEO performance also depends on content and links. [Google’s SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide#things-we-believe-you-shouldnt-focus-on)

## Research Scope

The research checked public search footprints, the domain-focused finalists’ current pages, registration records, and available archive snapshots. Those five finalists currently show parking pages.

A traffic or backlink advantage could not be established for any candidate. This is a ranking of **potential for this app**, rather than measured existing rankings.

## Domain Ranking

1. **domaindatabases.com** — **Best overall.** Clear connection to domain inventories, close to the current brand, and no hyphen. “Databases” being plural is its small weakness.
2. **domains-database.com** — Closest match to **Domains Database** and immediately descriptive. Slightly harder to dictate and remember because of the hyphen.
3. **domainnameplans.com** — Reasonably memorable and suitable for organizing domains around future projects. Could also sound like registration pricing plans.
4. **domainnameplanners.com** — Relevant to domain planning, but longer and sounds more like naming consultants than portfolio management software.
5. **sitesdatabase.com** — Short and clear for searchable collections. “Sites” implies existing websites, which makes it less precise for undeveloped domains and registrar management.
6. **websitesdatabase.com** — Very descriptive for a website directory, but long and broader than the app’s primary purpose.
7. **domainnamedevs.com** — Domain-focused, but suggests developers, a community, or development services rather than inventory and renewal tracking.
8. **websitesbase.com** — Usable for a collection platform, although the wording is less natural and its purpose needs explanation.
9. **sitesighter.com** — Distinctive, but does not communicate domain management. Its similarity to the existing analytics product **SiteSights** could make brand searches less distinct. This is an assessment of the names, not measured user confusion. [SiteSights](https://sitesights.io/)
10. **websighter.com** — Flexible branding, but unclear purpose and potential “WebSight/WebSighter” spelling confusion. Similar software branding already exists. [WebSight](https://www.websight.io/)
11. **forgotten-internet.com** — Stronger for discovering abandoned websites or internet history. It sets the wrong expectation for managing active domain portfolios.
12. **databasesdatabase.com** — **Weakest fit.** Repetitive wording, difficult recall, and suggests a directory of databases rather than domains.

## SEO Positioning

Position the homepage as:

> Domains Database — Domain Portfolio Manager & Renewal Tracker

Publish useful public pages about registrar synchronization, renewal tracking, and organizing domain portfolios. These describe the app’s actual value more precisely than “domain database” alone.

## Redirect Plan

Redirect the other domains directly to **domaindatabases.com** using server-side **301 or 308 redirects**, mapping existing pages to equivalent destinations.

Use the primary host consistently in canonical URLs and the sitemap. Redirects consolidate existing signals; simply owning extra keyword domains does not create additional search traffic. [Google’s redirect guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes)
